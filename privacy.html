Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PickScope ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website pickscope.com (the "Site").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the Site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out our contact form (name, email address, subject, message content)
  • Subscribe to our newsletter (email address)
  • Send us an email directly

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our Site:

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time and date of visits.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. See Section 6 for more details on cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send you newsletters and updates (only if you have opted in)
  • To improve our Site content, layout, and user experience
  • To analyze Site traffic and usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share information with third-party service providers who help us operate our Site (e.g., hosting providers, analytics services). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites, including Amazon and other affiliate partners. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

5.1 Amazon Associates Program

PickScope is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click on an Amazon link from our Site and make a purchase, Amazon may place cookies on your device to track the referral. Please refer to Amazon's privacy policy for information on how they handle your data.

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how you use our Site
  • Enable affiliate link tracking

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them. However, disabling cookies may affect your experience on our Site.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@pickscope.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
