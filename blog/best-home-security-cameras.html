📊 What to Look For

✅ Must-Haves

  • Prioritize models with local storage to avoid ongoing subscription fees
  • 2K resolution is the sweet spot — great quality without excessive bandwidth use
  • Dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz + 5GHz) support is essential
  • IP65 or higher water resistance for outdoor mounting
  • Check the camera's field of view — ultra-wide angles reduce blind spots

❌ What to Avoid

  • Don't be fooled by "1080p HD" — in 2026, 2K is the baseline
  • Don't overlook total storage costs — some brands have pricey subscriptions
  • Don't mount cameras where they invade neighbor privacy
  • Don't rely solely on battery-powered models for 24/7 continuous recording

💡 FAQ

Q: Wireless or wired?

Wireless cameras are easier to install but need periodic recharging. Wired cameras are ideal for 24/7 continuous recording. If you want a "set it and forget it" experience, solar-powered models like the EufyCam S330 are your best bet.

Q: Cloud storage or local storage?

Cloud storage is convenient but requires a subscription. Local storage saves money but you'll need to supply your own microSD card or hub. We recommend a model that supports both, giving you flexibility for different needs.
