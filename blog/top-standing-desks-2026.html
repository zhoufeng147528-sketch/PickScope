🔍 Review Criteria

  1. Stability (35% weight): How much the desk wobbles at maximum height — the single most important factor.

📊 Standing Desk Buying Guide

✅ What to Look For

  • Dual motor > single motor: more stable, faster, higher weight capacity
  • Go with a 60" x 30" desktop or larger if you need dual monitors plus a laptop
  • Prioritize collision detection to protect your desk and belongings
  • Memory height presets are incredibly useful — switch between sit and stand with one touch
  • Check that the min and max heights work for your height

❌ What to Avoid

  • Don't just look at the price tag — cheap single-motor desks wobble significantly at max height
  • Don't overlook desktop material — cheap particle board can warp over time
  • Watch the shipping weight — some desks need two people to carry
  • Don't forget about cable management

💡 FAQ

Q: Are standing desks actually good for your health?

Yes. Research shows that alternating between sitting and standing can reduce lower back pain, improve posture, and increase calorie burn. The key word is "alternating" — don't stand all day either. Aim to switch positions every 45-60 minutes.

Q: What's the difference between single and dual motors?

Dual-motor standing desks significantly outperform single-motor models in stability, lift speed, and weight capacity. If your budget allows, we strongly recommend going with a dual-motor model. The price difference is typically $100-$200, but the experience gap is substantial.
