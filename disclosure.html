Advertising Disclosure

Transparency is one of our core values

📢 Our Commitment to Transparency

At PickScope, we believe in being completely transparent with our readers about how we operate and generate revenue. This page explains how advertising and affiliate partnerships work on our Site, so you can make informed decisions about the content you consume.

🛒 Amazon Associates Program

PickScope is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. This means that when you click on a link to Amazon (or other Amazon properties) from our Site and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission — at no additional cost to you.

How It Works

  1. We write detailed, honest product reviews and buying guides.
  2. Within our content, we include links to products available on Amazon and other retailers.
  3. If you click one of these links and make a purchase within a certain timeframe (typically 24 hours), we earn a small commission from the retailer.
  4. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the retailer.

What This Means for You

  • No extra cost: Using our affiliate links does not increase the price you pay for any product.
  • Support our work: When you purchase through our links, you help support our independent testing and content creation efforts — at zero cost to you.
  • We don't know who you are: We do not receive any personally identifiable information about you from the affiliate programs. We only know that someone clicked a link and made a purchase.

🔒 Our Editorial Independence

We want to be absolutely clear on this point: our affiliate relationships do not influence our product rankings, reviews, or recommendations in any way.

  • We do not accept payment from manufacturers for favorable reviews or higher rankings.
  • Products are selected for review based on market popularity, reader interest, and editorial judgment — never based on affiliate commission rates.
  • The affiliate commission rate for a product plays zero role in where it appears on our "best of" lists or how highly we rate it.
  • If a product is the best, we will recommend it — regardless of whether it has an affiliate program or not.

📊 Other Affiliate Programs

In addition to the Amazon Associates Program, we may participate in other affiliate programs, including but not limited to:

  • Walmart Affiliate Program
  • Best Buy Affiliate Program
  • Target Affiliate Program
  • ShareASale
  • Impact / Impact Radius
  • CJ Affiliate (formerly Commission Junction)

The same principles apply to all affiliate programs we participate in: our editorial independence is never compromised.

📢 Display Advertising

We may also display non-intrusive advertisements on our Site through advertising networks such as Google AdSense, Mediavine, or similar platforms. These ads are typically marked as "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" to distinguish them from our editorial content.

📝 Sponsored Content

On rare occasions, we may publish sponsored content or paid partnerships with brands. When we do, the content will be clearly and prominently labeled as "Sponsored," "Paid Partnership," or similar. We will never present paid content as if it were independent editorial content.

❓ Why We Use Affiliate Links

Creating high-quality, in-depth product reviews takes a significant amount of time and resources. Our team spends hundreds of hours researching, testing, and writing about products. Affiliate commissions allow us to:

  • Purchase products for testing (we buy most of the products we review ourselves)
  • Pay our writers, editors, and testers fairly for their work
  • Keep our content free for all readers — no paywalls, no subscriptions
  • Invest in better testing equipment and methodologies

✅ Our Promise to You

We make the following promises to every PickScope reader:

  1. Honesty first: We will always give you our honest opinion, even if it means we earn less (or nothing) from a recommendation.
  2. Transparency always: We clearly disclose our affiliate relationships. If there's a commission opportunity, you'll know about it.
  3. No pay-for-play: We will never accept payment in exchange for a favorable review or higher ranking.
  4. Reader-first: Every recommendation we make is designed to help you find the best product for your needs and budget — not to maximize our affiliate income.

📬 Questions?

If you have any questions about our advertising disclosure, affiliate relationships, or how we handle editorial integrity, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're happy to answer any questions you may have.
